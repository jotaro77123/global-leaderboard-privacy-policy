Delete Your Global Leaderboard Account

You can delete your account and associated data directly within the app:

  1. Open the Global Leaderboard app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile information, ratings, achievements, statistics, streaks, friends list, and progress.

What may be retained

Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate operational purposes, including fraud prevention and security, consistent with our Privacy Policy.
